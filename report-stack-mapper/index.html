How to read the map

The useful parts are the places where trust can break.

Once the reports are visible, the first pilot can define required versus optional sources, arrival windows, business-date rules, and what the brief should say when the morning is incomplete.

Required sourceIf it is missing, dependent KPIs should block or be clearly blanked.Trust
Optional sourceIf it is late, the output can ship with a visible caveat and rerun path.Caveat
Manual sourceIf a person edits it, the pilot needs approval rules and handoff notes.Owner
Wrong date riskBusiness-date checks should happen before any AI summary or recommendation.Blocker
